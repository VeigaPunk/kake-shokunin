## Verdict

**Holds.** No run exceeds 30 s wall or 500 MB peak RSS — the worst case observed is
0.68 s and 84 MiB (50000 findings), 44x under the time gate and ~6x under the memory
gate. Nothing to report at the acceptance threshold.

Observations short of a finding:

- Deep nesting is safe: CPython `html.parser` is iterative, and the refs placed at
  depth 20000 were still seen (referencesChecked=2), so the scan is not an early exit.
  No recursion blow-up and no stack risk surfaced at 20000 levels.
- The 50000-finding run shows where memory actually goes: peak RSS roughly doubles
  (43.9 → 86.1 MiB) and the printed JSON is 12438978 bytes; the finding `id` strings
  (`scripts/base_path_audit.py`, `evaluate_page`: `f"{page['file']}#{index}:…"`) and
  result serialization dominate. Scaling is linear in findings, bounded by the 2 MiB
  page cap, so a 500 MB blow-up is not reachable through one page.
- `_validate_manifest`'s duplicate check (`any(item["file"] == … for item in
  normalized_pages)`) is O(n²) in page count, but the 1 MiB manifest cap bounds n at
  ~11000 pages; measured in-contract worst case costs 2.63 s total including reading
  11000 files. Bounded by design, not a defect at the contract edge.
