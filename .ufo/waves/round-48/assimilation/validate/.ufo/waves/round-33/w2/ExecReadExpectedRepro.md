## Interpretation and limits

The base reader catches OSError from `os.open` and converts it to `ValueError`, then rejects directories/FIFOs as non-regular; its caller catches only `ValueError` (`base-clone/scripts/citing_set_selftest.py:399-423,544-548`). The tip moves descriptor `fstat`/read/close inside that OSError conversion and adds a caller OSError handler (`clone/scripts/citing_set_selftest.py:435-459,581-588`; repair diff inspected with `git show e4903817669aa411e424006bb342b345e143ab20 -- scripts/citing_set_selftest.py`). Thus mode 000 and dangling-link errors occur at open and were already handled by base; directory and FIFO are rejected before reading. **Risk/limit:** no tested shape induced an OSError after a successful open, so this reproduction does not validate that precise repair path.
