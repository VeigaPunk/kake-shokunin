Create, personalize, and launch beautiful websites

From any input to a live website

Kimi Websites transforms your inputs into fully structured sites from a quick description, a photo, or a video clip. Recreate existing pages from screenshots or screen recordings with precision, keeping layouts, interactions, and design intact, without setup steps.

Stunning design, right from the start

Every site is built with a designer's eye. Be it bold hero sections, fluid interactive elements, or smooth scroll animations, Kimi Websites can deliver. Expect clean, well-composed layouts that rival award-winning websites, making every page impossible to ignore.

Real websites that work end-to-end

More than just good looks. Kimi Websites delivers structured frontends paired with lightweight backend logic, covering databases, user authentication, and interactive functionality. Spin up complete, multi-page sites that are built to grow, taking you from a simple concept to a fully operational product.

Edit and customize through conversation

Tell Kimi what you want, and watch it happen. Type changes in plain language, whether you’re overhauling an entire section or fine-tuning a single button. Prefer something more hands-on? Switch to visual editing, circle the element you want to change, and give your instruction. Tweak themes, fonts, layouts, and styles until the site looks exactly the way you envisioned.

Make live and share your site within clicks

Every site built with Kimi Websites is ready to go live right out of the box with a fully responsive design for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices without lifting a finger. Hit publish with a single click and share your link straight away. From your first prompt to a live website, everything happens in one seamless place.
