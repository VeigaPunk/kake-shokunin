readings
- Page-chrome blanking is what makes a heading span page-unique on docs.github.com: "About publishing sources"
  occurs in one text node of the article but in two nodes of the raw page (a navigation copy). Without the
  exclusion the counter would fail an honest quote; with it, the article occurrence is the unique one.
- Real single-node spans exist and are unique (13-unit example above), so the C3 rule is satisfiable on this
  page for a careful author.
- Spans that cross inline markup (links, `<code>`, `<strong>`) are not one text node and fail C3 with the note
  "(it occurs only across inline markup)" — measured, so the note is not hypothetical.
- Entities are not decoded: a quote containing a quotation mark appears in the body as `&quot;`, and a literal
  ASCII double quote cannot appear inside a Gate double-quoted span at all.
