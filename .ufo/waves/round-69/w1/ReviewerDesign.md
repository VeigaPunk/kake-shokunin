At 4d188f94, `git show 4d188f94:scripts/freshness-sweep.mjs | grep -n '/tmp'` → 6 lines: comments 20, 103; **code 108, 182, 899, 1204**. Line 182 is the interior of the `usage()` template literal (`console.log(\`freshness-sweep — …` at 171): `--selftest hermetic fixtures under /tmp/kake-r16/selftest; prints a` — a non-comment code line carrying a /tmp path literal. Line 899 (`pq12.r15Snapshot = { … skipped: 'snapshot absent (expected once landed: /tmp is not durable)' }`) carries '/tmp' as prose, counted under substring matching, not under "path literal". So the definition yields **3** clear sites (108, 182, 1204) — 4 under substring — never 2. The claim's "(two sites at base: line 108, line 1204)" and the control "Base measurement: tmp_literals=2" are wrong as written; any counter printing 2 at base is not implementing the frozen sentence. Fix (minimal): definition → "code lines containing the substring `/tmp`" (yields 4 at base) and correct the claim parenthetical to four sites (20/103 comments excluded); ExecC2Fix must then also reword the help text at 182 and the prose at 899, else the predicate can never reach 0.
