## Verdict on the claim

- **Satisfiable:** yes — demonstrated `met=true` end-to-end (real capture, worktree mode) and, independently, via a wholly fabricated capture (F1), so the mechanics cannot be satisfiable-only-on-paper.
- **Measures what it claims:** the predicate measures re-derivation and internal consistency of the committed chain — receipt digests, byte counts, declared-surface equality, allowlist, audit determinism — plus *self-declared* freshness. It does not and cannot measure that a fresh GET actually happened this round (no witness exists offline); F2 makes even the freshness check order-wrong across timezones; F3 makes the audit-equality binding revision-dependent. Within its honest scope ("re-derives from committed bytes"), the v2 counter is sound and its controls reproduce; the capability row should record that scope, F4/F5 should land before closure, and carrier scoring should use `--revision`.
