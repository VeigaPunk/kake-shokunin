# MutC — round 84 wave w1

## Axes and method
- Axes: guard discrimination, fixture fidelity, exact restoration.
- Six independent guard-removal mutants were evaluated concurrently in isolated scratch copies; three per counter.
- No production, frozen, sibling, fleet-root, or VCS state was modified. No network or tool installation was used.
- Every probe ran the identical bounded detector on pristine, mutant, and restored counter bytes: counter selftest, positive fixture, and targeted negative fixture.
- Each subprocess had a 60-second timeout. Every baseline selftest and positive fixture exited 0; every baseline negative fixture exited 1. No baseline was inconclusive.
- C1 positive fixtures use the frozen counter's fixture builders. This shares fixture construction with its selftest, rather than providing wholly independent fixtures.
- C2 fixtures copy retained capture A/B, both frozen synthetic pairs, declarations, expectations, and the actual policy tool plus its sibling classifier into scratch.
- The C2 positive record was generated by the copied actual tool (exit 0), not mocked. It is a constructed positive fixture, not evidence that the lane's committed record or after-state passes.
- Each negative fixture changes only the relevant input; frozen lane files remain untouched. Fixture construction and every exact command are retained in the receipt driver and JSON.
