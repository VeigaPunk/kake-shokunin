## Three most important lessons / gains

1. **The lane stopped chasing the URL-check cost claim and turned the failure into a gate.** Rounds 42 and 43 both ended NOT MET even though the build was correct and fast (T1 about 1.0x and T5 about 1.1–1.2x the old tool, against 4.2 and 7.05 for the do-nothing control). Each counter revision (v3 at `1e1da69`, v4 at `b49cba9`) was beaten by new controls: 4 under v3 and 14 under v4. Round 44 changed the question. Instead of a fifth counter, it built a witness-class gate (`c44_count_v5.py`, `c44_audit.py`, `classes/gN_*.py`) and a placebo copy whose class modules do nothing. The gate certifies all 27 round-43 controls as witnessed and the placebo as 0/27, so you can now tell why a control met. That was the first admitted operability move since round 36. The lesson is that after two rounds of counter patching fail, you build an instrument that explains the failures instead of adding more patches.

2. **Receipts became the thing being audited.** Round 45's driver v2 (`dc7f03e`) refuses receipts with a bad counter exit code, a "tool absent" note, or a timestamp outside the run window (invalid accepted went from 3 to 0, while the honest cohort stayed at 27/27 and the build at 3/3). It also found that two of the lane's own standing claims were false: the shipped selftest killed only 2/4 mutants, and a v4 receipt named runs that v4 never executes. Round 46's `scripts/citation_lint.py` (`ee43e66`) found 28 dead or ambiguous short-hash citations in LOG.md and SCORECARD.md, and every one is now adjudicated in `corpus/quality/citation-adjudications.md`. Round 47 then repaired round 46's own record. The ledger has 20 rows, not 12. Five reason cells were wrong. One "offline sweep exit 0" claim had no receipt, so it was withdrawn rather than backfilled after the fact (LOG `[r47-correction R6 withdrawal]`). Knowledge entries `receipt-integrity.md` and `commit-citation-hygiene.md` (S133, S136) record the rules. Self-reporting is now checked by tools, not left to reviewers.

3. **First admitted capability on João's actual hosting surface since round 36, but its own attackers say it is shallow.** `scripts/redirect_audit.py` is an offline, read-only audit of static-host redirect and rewrite rules (resource precedence, first-match rules, splats, default status). It is grounded in Render (S050) and Cloudflare Pages (S135) docs and comes with a sealed matrix (`c46_matrix.py`, selftest 23/23). It closes ground that PROVINCE has listed as knowledge-only since round 30. However, the round-46 residual attack reports, re-opened at the tip of round 47, find real gaps.
